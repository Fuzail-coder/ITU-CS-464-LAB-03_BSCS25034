2. Explanation of the Level and Its Obstacles
The blockout is built around a single continuous mountain range that separates a starting runway from open sea. Grey-boxed, low-poly peaks of varying height and width stand in for the final art and already define the playable flight corridor, so the level's difficulty comes entirely from the terrain rather than from scripted hazards.
Stars as collectible objectives
•	The level starts from a runway, giving the player a safe area to take off and build speed. 
•	A mountain range creates the main obstacle, requiring the player to fly over or between the peaks. 
•	Stars are placed around the mountains, encouraging players to take risks while collecting them. 
•	After the mountains, the player reaches the open sea, providing a clear escape route and ending the challenging section. 
Controls: W = Nose Up | S = Nose Down | A/D = Left/Right | Shift = Fast | Space = Accelerate
